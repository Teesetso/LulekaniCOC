About Us — Church of Christ

Learn more about who we are, what we believe, and our purpose as a congregation of God's people.

🏛️ Who We Are

We are a congregation of the Church of Christ, committed to following the teachings and pattern of the New Testament. We worship God in spirit and in truth, live faithfully according to His Word, and share the Gospel with our community.

We are not part of any denomination nor guided by human creeds. We simply seek to be Christians only, as the Bible teaches.

📖 What We Believe

  • The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our only guide for faith and practice.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Lord and Savior.
  • Salvation comes through hearing, believing, repenting, confessing Christ, and being baptized by immersion (Acts 2:38).
  • Worship must be simple, scriptural, and focused on God.
  • We sing acapella (without instruments) as taught in the New Testament (Ephesians 5:19).
  • We partake of the Lord’s Supper every Sunday (Acts 20:7).
  • Each congregation is independent and led by local leaders.
  • We strive to live holy, loving, and faithful lives as disciples of Jesus.

🎯 Our Mission

  • Teach and uphold the truth of God’s Word
  • Lead souls to Christ through the Gospel
  • Strengthen and encourage the church family
  • Serve our community with love and compassion
  • Glorify God in everything we do

Our goal is to help people grow spiritually and become faithful followers of Christ.

🌟 Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered, Bible-based congregation that shines God's light in our community, equips believers, and helps all people develop a deeper relationship with God.

🕊️ What to Expect When You Visit

  • A warm and friendly welcome
  • Congregational acapella singing
  • Prayer and Bible-based preaching
  • The Lord’s Supper every Sunday
  • A simple, reverent worship service
  • A loving and caring church family

📜 Our History

The Church of Christ follows the pattern of the New Testament church. Our local congregation began with a small group of believers who desired to worship God according to the Bible. Over the years, we have continued to grow in faith, love, and fellowship while remaining committed to the teachings of Christ.
